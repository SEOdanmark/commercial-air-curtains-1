Commercial air curtains provide businesses with an effective method of separating indoor and outdoor environments while keeping entrances accessible throughout normal operating hours. Flexiheat UK supplies commercial air curtains designed for retail shops, restaurants, hotels, commercial offices, reception areas, supermarkets, sports centres, warehouses, food service premises and many other commercial applications. By producing a controlled stream of air across a doorway, commercial air curtains establish an invisible air barrier that can help reduce heat loss, retain conditioned air, limit cold draughts and reduce the movement of dust, debris and flying insects through an open entrance.

Water flow through the commercial air curtain can be controlled through an on/off valve system. This allows the heating function to respond to the requirements of the commercial premises rather than operating continuously when heating is unnecessary.

Butchers, food processing areas and other food-related businesses may therefore consider commercial air curtains as part of their wider entrance management strategy. The air barrier does not replace normal hygiene measures, but it can provide additional environmental separation.

commercial air curtain heaters

Commercial air curtains can additionally reduce the movement of flying insects through open restaurant entrances. The continuous stream of air provides an additional obstacle for insects attempting to enter the building. Commercial air curtains can therefore complement the wider hygiene and environmental controls used within food service premises.

The same commercial air curtains can provide environmental separation during warmer weather. In an air-conditioned shop, office, hotel, restaurant or other commercial building, cooled indoor air can escape whenever an external doorway remains open. At the same time, warmer external air can enter the premises. A commercial air curtain helps separate these temperature zones while allowing unrestricted access.

Commercial air curtain controls can further support efficient operation. Intelligent controls can adjust fan operation and heating according to demand. This means the commercial air curtain does not necessarily need to operate at maximum output under every condition.

Maintenance also affects long-term commercial air curtain performance. Air inlets need to remain clear so the fans can draw sufficient air through the unit. Dust accumulation can reduce airflow and place unnecessary demand on components.

The heating requirement should then be considered. If the building has a suitable hot water heating system, the W water-heated commercial air curtain may provide an appropriate solution. Where direct electrical heating is preferred, the E electric commercial air curtain can provide heated airflow. Where heating is unnecessary, the C ambient commercial air curtain can establish the air barrier without additional heat.

The objective is to match commercial air curtain operation with actual building requirements. Providing unnecessary heating increases energy consumption, while insufficient airflow reduces the effectiveness of the air barrier.

commercial overdoor heater

The heating output of water-heated commercial air curtains depends on the flow and return temperatures of the water supplied to the heat exchanger. Higher water temperatures can provide greater available heating output, while lower-temperature systems require appropriate commercial air curtain selection according to the desired performance.

The E commercial air curtain is an electrically heated model. Electric heating elements warm the airflow passing through the commercial air curtain before it is discharged across the entrance. This provides warm air at the doorway without requiring a connection to a hot water heating circuit.

The positioning of commercial air curtains contributes to their performance. Horizontal commercial air curtains should normally be installed close to the doorway so the airflow begins separating the environments immediately at the entrance.

Flexiheat UK supplies commercial air curtains designed to achieve this combination of environmental separation, heating options and unrestricted access. Water-heated, electrically heated and ambient commercial air curtains allow businesses to choose equipment suited to their existing building services and operating requirements.

Energy consumption is an important reason businesses consider commercial air curtains. Heating or cooling air requires energy. When conditioned indoor air leaves through an open doorway, some of that energy is effectively lost from the building.

A commercial air curtain designed for a standard shop doorway should not automatically be assumed suitable for a larger warehouse opening. Installation height, width, external wind pressure and internal building pressure should all be considered when specifying commercial air curtains for these applications.

During winter, the heating system maintains the required internal temperature while the commercial air curtain helps prevent some of that heated air from escaping. During summer, the air-conditioning system cools the premises while the commercial air curtain helps retain the conditioned air.

Electric commercial air curtains can be particularly suitable for premises where an appropriate electrical supply is available or where extending the building's hot water system to the entrance is not practical. The maximum installation height for the electrically heated commercial air curtain is approximately 3.7 metres.

The result is a flexible range of commercial air curtains suitable for many commercial environments. Retail shops are a common location for commercial air curtains because customer access and energy management often need to be balanced. An open shop door can make a retail premises easily accessible, but it also allows indoor air to escape continuously.

Flexiheat UK commercial air curtains provide options for these different requirements. The commercial air curtain range can accommodate horizontal mounting heights of up to approximately 4 metres with ambient models and approximately 3.7 metres with heated configurations.

Commercial air curtains help reduce this unwanted air exchange. The potential energy savings depend on the dimensions of the doorway, how frequently it is open, the difference between indoor and outdoor temperatures, external wind conditions and the efficiency of the existing heating or cooling system.
